For every project this is going to be different but generally you need to request from your client the following:

# [[Collect Legal, Logos, and Supers from client|Legal, Logos, and Supers]]
# Creative Brief/Deck
# Scope of work
# [[CAD Asset requirements and standards|CAD files of product]]
# Art files with textures
# [[Client product standards and best practices|Request of physical version of the product]]
# Visual references or creative direction
# List of contacts for project approvals
# [[Collect Deliverable specifications from client|List of deliverables that will be need to be made]]
# Schedule for delivery/air date

Most of these items will have to be delivered after project kick offs.
