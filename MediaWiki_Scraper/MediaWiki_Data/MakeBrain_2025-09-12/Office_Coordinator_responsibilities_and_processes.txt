
= Office Coordinator responsibilities and processes =
Different spheres of the Office Coordinator role:

'''Administrative Assistance'''

* outfitting new hires with key fobs and granting fobs appropriate access (freight elevator/bicycle storage/fitness center)
* receiving mail, sending packages
* answering the phone

'''Office/Building Operations'''

* office space management & organization ''-''  keep lobby spotless, declutter storage spaces, stock fridges, buy groceries and misc. office supplies, run dishwashers, clean messes, organize repair for appliances
* coordinate with building maintenance - repairs, render farm, amenities forms, loading dock forms
* recycling and sometimes trash removal

* storage stewardship - understand various storage areas, put things in storage and be able to retrieve them

'''Employee Engagement'''

* birthday and anniversary recognition
* Friday Viewing Fiesta catering
'''On Set P.A.'''
* helping out with shoots
* stage room maintenance
* taking care of film equipment

=== Administrative Assistance ===
Important forms

'''Answering the phone'''

If a call rings on Line 1, answer as soon as possible and at least within three rings. If a call rings on Line 4 (Danny's line), allow two rings for Danny to pick up before picking up.

You can quickly talk to Danny, Oren, Matt, and Elisabeth from the front desk by dialing their extension. If the mic is turned on, they will be able to hear you without you having to use the phone receiver. In general, the mic on the front desk phone is left on (as indicated by a red light).

To answer the phone, pick up the receiver and say a greeting ("Hello, thank you for calling MAKE. This is ___ speaking. What can I do for you?"). If the caller is trying to reach a specific person in the office, get the caller's name and the company they're calling with. Put the caller on hold (just press Hold button), then dial the requested person at MAKE using their extension. Ask if they would like to speak to the person on the line, and if not, you can take the caller's contact info and a message.

To take a caller off hold, just press the button for the line they're on (typically Line 1).

To make a call, first press the button for Line 1, then dial the number.

'''Loading Dock & Recycling Center'''

The loading dock is useful for large deliveries. Instacart shoppers will sometimes like to utilize the loading dock, but they can get confused how to get to our office from the loading dock, so assistance is almost always needed in these cases. During shoots, craft services will typically use the loading dock first thing in the morning.

Whenever we use the loading dock, we need to submit a loading dock form.

When we have furniture deliveries from Room & Board, we will need to set up an appointment with the building management (notify then in advance).
