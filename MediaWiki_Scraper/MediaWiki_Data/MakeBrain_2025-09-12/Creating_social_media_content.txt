== What is "Content Creation" in social media? ==
Whether you're creating MAKE content, Client content or MAKE Original content — social media content is a valuable asset regarding ''what, why'' and ''for whom:''

==== ''what, why'' and ''for whom:'' ====
* '''''WHAT'':''' Our social platforms give MAKE visibility and a voice.
* '''''WHY:''''' By creating a social media ''content strategy'', we are able to turn to our social media followers for insight about everything from popular content ideas, building brand loyalty and increasing overall reach.
* '''''FOR WHOM:''''' When we put valuable efforts into our social media content strategy, we will be able to see the pieces that had the most success on the platforms by watching the likes/comments ''(i.e analytics)'' as well as the traffic and shares within the platform. Since social media offers potentially millions of visitors, it is the most accessible place to increase brand recognition and reputation.
