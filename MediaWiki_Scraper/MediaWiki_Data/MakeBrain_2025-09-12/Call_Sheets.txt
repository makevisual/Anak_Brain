Call sheets are created for each prelight day and each shoot day in a shoot. They are sent in an email before the shoot/prelight day (by noon the day before). At the end of the email, ask for confirmation that the recipients have received the email.

Call sheets must be sent to everyone on the crew, talent and talent guardians, talent agents, and clients.
