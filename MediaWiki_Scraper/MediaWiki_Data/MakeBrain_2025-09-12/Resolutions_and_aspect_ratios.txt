=Templates=
Safe Frame templates will be copied into all new project folders when they are created, you can find them in the Assets\Images folder.
	<ul>
	<li>''Projects\[Project}\Assets\Images\SafeFrames\''
	</li>
	</ul>

These should be brought in and overlayed on your edit at the start of the project. Title Safes are particularly important in broadcast edits.
