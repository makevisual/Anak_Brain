To begin the edit phase of the project we need to get the following information from the client.

* Legal Line - This is necessary for TVC deliveries
* Logos - This is necessary for all island pieces
* Supers - If there are any specific supers they have or information/translations that we are required to put on screen

===Legal Lines===
Legal lines will range from the legal text at the bottom of an end island to the legal disclaimers we need to include on shots with special effects. This wording is specific for each client and to get this, we ask ahead of the edit but really we ask the client to send the first rough edit to their legal team for clearance. Then their legal team will give us the exact wording we need.

===Logos===
These are to be supplied by the client each time. In the End Island example above, you can see feature logo, product logo (lower left), and company logo were all supplied to us. Unless the project is about logo creation, we generally always ask for logos to be sent to us in a keyable format and if there is any animation with it, to be supplied animating and still as well.


===Supers===
We commonly have to create Supers but many times the text inside the super has to be specific. It is best practice to always ask and check with you client what they need and would like in the supers on screen. This typically is a good question to ask before the rough edit 1 but commonly will be one that changes as the project goes on through the phases.
