=Introduction=

This page contains step by step instructions on how to upload MAKE's content to the MAKE Vimeo Page.

=Process=
'''Step By Step'''
<ol>
	<li>Log into Vimeo
	</li>
[[File:How_to_update_Vimeo_Login.JPG|450px]]
*If you have access to MAKE’s Vimeo make sure to select the MAKE user
[[File:How_to_update_Vimeo_Select_User.JPG|450px]]
	<li>Expand Library Tab 
	</li>
[[File:How_to_update_Vimeo_Select_Library.JPG|450px]]
	<li>Drag the video you want to upload to the correct tab.
	</li>
*'''MAKE Hidden''' for sharing (Remains hidden on the MAKE site)
*'''MAKE Originals''' for Hosting Original work
*'''MAKE Commercials''' for projects that will be live on the site.

	<li>Once uploaded – click the video and select settings and hit Advanced Settings.
	</li>
[[File:How_to_update_Vimeo_Advanced_Settings.JPG|317x317px]]
    <li>Rename the Video 
	</li>
*Rename the Video - Client - name of spot
</ol>
 <nowiki>
Example name: Nebraska Lottery - Bored Snowman </nowiki>

<ol>
    <li>Add Description
	</li>

    <li>Select a thumbnail form the actual video. Make sure it’s a well composed and interesting image. Not the generic first second.
	</li>

    <li>Hit Save and you are done.
	</li>

    <li>Making a video public.
	</li>
*Select the hide from Vimeo Tag 
[[File:How_to_update_Vimeo_Publishing_Tab.JPG|450px]]
*Select Public
[[File:How_to_update_Vimeo_Publishing_Public.JPG|376x376px]]
</ol>
