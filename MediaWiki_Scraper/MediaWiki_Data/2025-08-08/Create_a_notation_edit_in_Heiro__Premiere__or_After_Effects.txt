=Create a notation edit in Heiro=

When you first create edit for project, start by opening BurnIn Preset

"U:\resources\Presets\Foundry Hiero\Hiero_BurnInPresets_v001.hrox"

Save edit to your project's edit folder right away, so you don't accidently overwrite preset.


Preset file already has preset for exporting shots and sequences.


To export shots, reference, 

[[Building a shot structure in Heiro|Building a shot structure in Heiro]]


To render sequences, reference, 

[[Creating Masters, Super-less Masters, and Graphics Only Edits|Creating Masters, Super-less Masters, and Graphics Only Edits]]

[[Export and share a daily|Export and share a daily]]


Drag renders below BurnIn and Safechart to see overlay in action.

[[File:Create a notation edit in Heiro, Premiere, or After Effects Hiero Layers 001.jpg|450px]]


If you are working in resoultion other than HD, you can double click BurnIn to change text scale to better fit your resolution.

[[File:Create a notation edit in Heiro, Premiere, or After Effects BurnIn Property 001.jpg|450px]]


===Save BurnIn from Preset===

You can save BurnIn setting from preset file by clicking wrench icon then "Save as Preset"

[[File:Create a notation edit in Heiro, Premiere, or After Effects Create BurnIn 000.jpg|450px]]

Now BurnIn preset is stored in your computer. 

When you want to create BurnIn layer you can select empty layer, click on "Add effects" Icon then click on BurnIn

[[File:Create a notation edit in Heiro, Premiere, or After Effects Create BurnIn 001.jpg|450px]]


By default, it will bring Hiero default setting but by clicking wrench icon again, you will be able to bring Make preset we saved.

[[File:Create a notation edit in Heiro, Premiere, or After Effects Create BurnIn 002.jpg|450px]]
