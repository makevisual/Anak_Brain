In order to simplify the creation of BTS and Breakdown videos after projects complete, without needing to explicitly go back and 're-record' certain actions, we should be recording and storing videos of working on scenes/assets as we go.

Follow this article to install and use OBS for recording:

[[https://makevisual.com/brain/mediawiki/index.php?title=Workflow_-_How_and_When_to_use_OBS_for_recording_meetings|Using OBS to record your Screen]]

Store all recordings in your project in this location:<blockquote>..\Assets\Promotional\Content\Videos</blockquote>

== When and what to record: ==
We want to record 2-3 minutes of all/most KEY phases of a project. We do not want hours of footage, but enough that we can reference any part of the process and have footage to suit. Record a few minutes and name your files in a way the describes their content.

-Storyboarding

-Design

-Modeling

-Texturing

-Rigging (2d or 3d)

-Layout (2d or 3d)

-Animating (2d or 3d)

-Lighting/Painting

-Comping
