=Where to save models=

Always merge in models to scene you are working on. Do not open files in U Drive as starting point.

Many assets in U Drive has different Unit setup than our default 3D scene so always merge in 3d assets to your working file.


Bring textures to your asset's surfaces foler under Asset's maker (ex/ W:\Projects\BraveHurts\Assets\Characters\DAM_Damsel\Surfaces\Evermotion)

This will help you organize your textures.


=How to relink textures=

When you merge U drive assets to your scene, texture links will be broken.

In 3Ds max, Open "External Tools" panel then select "RelinkBitmaps"

[[File:Creating Resources - Models Relink Textures 01.jpg|1000px]]


You will see all the missing textures. click on "Browse" and go to folder where you copied your textures over to.

[[File:Creating Resources - Models Relink Textures 02.jpg|1000px]]


Click "Select Folder" Then Click on Relink button to relink textures to working project folder.
