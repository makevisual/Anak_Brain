== <big>General Wardrobe/Hair & Makeup Process</big> ==

=== First Steps ===

*Meet with Director/Creative Director for a preliminary discussion about anticipated wardrobe needs.
*Request and collect wardrobe direction information from client. Things like the season of the year the clothing should be, colors to use or avoid, if they have any inspiration pictures they could share. 
*Be sure to ask the client if they have any specific Hair & Makeup (HMU) requests so that the HMU person has time to buy products and is prepared before meeting with the talent at the fitting. 
* Book wardrobe Person (Discuss the budgeted number of shop/return days)
* Schedule Fitting 
* Talent Measurements should be collected either at casting (MN talent) or requested from agent at time of hold/book (OOT talent).

==== Special Considerations ====

#Do not include images of items that are unavailable or do not come in the size grouping of the talent that will be chosen for the project.
# Season of the year spot will be released.
# Types of shots and Activity of talent in the spot. (i.e. will we see shoes, are there hand close up shots, etc.)
# Will the shoot be on Green or Blue Screen.
=== Create updated Wardrobe Moodboard for Wardrobe Stylist & HMU ''[4]'' ===
[[File:HWMT CrushzillaSocial WardrobeMoodboard 02.png|thumb|'''''[4]''''' Example of initial Wardrobe Moodboard sent to Crew ]]
==== Information to Add to the Final Client Wardrobe Moodboard ====

# Talent Headshot, Measurements, Age and Contact information
# Web links for all images included
# All written client feedback (including notes about what client does not like).
# What type of shoot (Green Screen, Blue Screen, Location, etc.)
# Current Animatic Links
# Information about any wardrobe in Talent sizes already owned by MAKE.
# Information about any preliminary online wardrobe orders that have been done. (i.e. difficult/specific items)
=== Create and Communicate Fitting Plan/Schedule ===
*Create Load-in plan for Fitting
* Communicate Fitting plan with Wardrobe person (schedule of the day, how many outfit options will be sent to client, who will be taking photos, etc.)
* Be sure that HMU knows and is prepared for any specific client requests so they are prepared with the correct supplies when meeting with the talent at the fitting. 

=== Create and Communicate Shoot Plan/Schedule ===
*Create Load in plan for shoot
* Communicate "First Shot" + "Ready By" Expectations to HMU + Wardrobe
