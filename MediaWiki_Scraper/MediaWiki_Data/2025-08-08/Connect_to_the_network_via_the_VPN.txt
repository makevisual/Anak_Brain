== New VPN Client - 2/25/2025 ==
As of 2/24/2025 SonicWall no longer offers Sonicwall Mobile Connect. Nic is in the process of updating this article with new screenshots to reflect the new VPN client instructions. For now, basic instructions are below.

# Install the NetExtender VPN client from SonicWall's website. The current link is: [https://www.sonicwall.com/products/remote-access/vpn-clients SonicWall VPN Clients]
## Select the correct version for your computer. If you use Windows and you are unsure which option to choose, the 64-Bit MSI is likely the best choice.
# Once installed the NetExtender client should launch automatically. Click "Add connection" to create the VPN connection. This step is only done on the first launch.
## In the Add Connection window enter the following in the corresponding box:
### '''Name:''' Make
### '''Server:''' 50.249.104.46:4433
## Click Next
## On the Security Alert window click '''Trust'''.
## Additional boxes will become available on the Add Connection screen. Enter the following information into the corresponding box:
### '''Username:''' Your VPN Username. If you do not have this, contact Nic or Aaron.
### '''Password:''' Your VPN Password. If you do not have this, contact Nic or Aaron.
### '''Domain:''' Select "Make" from the dropdown box.
### Click '''Save.'''
## Click '''Save.'''
# On the main NetExtender window, click '''Connect.'''
# Once connected you should be able to work as though you were onsite at the office. Network drives and resources should be accessible.
