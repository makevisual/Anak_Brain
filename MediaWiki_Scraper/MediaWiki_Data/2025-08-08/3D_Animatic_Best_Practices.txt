=What is a 3D Animatic?=
3D animatics are a collection of unrendered shots in video format. They often serve the purpose of acting as a first step of visualization from a script or 2D storyboards. 3D animatics can contain CG shots, live action shots that will be filmed later on in the production, or a combination of both. They will often feature placeholder assets or simpler versions of the product or characters that will be replaced in later iterations of the spot.

Think of it as the building blocks for the spot, where each shot will eventually be upgraded and replaced with better animation, actual rendered shots, and high quality visuals.

This article will explain best practices to utilize while you create an animatic to ensure you're building a strong foundation for the spot during production.

== Creating the Edit ==

If there are multiple artists working on an animatic there will be only one person "running the edit".
It is recommended to compile your shots as you create them to ensure they flow together nicely. Your "Edit" file is where this will happen, and will contain your shots, scratch VO, music, any necessary sound effects, and supers or other supplementary visual effects.
