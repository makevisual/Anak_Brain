Struggling with social media content ideas? Here's a list that breaks down what content is typically showcased on our MAKE Original social medias when we launch a new episode or series.

===== Animation Process Breakdowns =====
The animation process breakdown video will start with the final render as the first frame (since we want to use this frame as our thumbnail), then progress to the '''rough animatic''', '''clean line''', '''colored version''', and '''finally return to the final render.''' The video should loop smoothly!

''Ex:'' [https://www.instagram.com/p/DAt3_Q-y0m1/ Dead Moon Walking - Animation Process Breakdown - Bogey Blunders]

''Ex: [https://www.instagram.com/p/CsFHsFGPn8p/ Mechsplorers - Animation Process Breakdown]''



===== Character Design Sheets/Poses =====
For the character design sheets and poses post, we can either share them as still images or as a video. If we opt for still images, we need to ensure that the first images in the Instagram carousel look cohesive and compatible with other posts in the feed. Alternatively, if we choose to create a video, we can use existing transition and animation assets from the series to smoothly reveal the character design sheets and poses. Once we've showcased the design sheets and character poses, we should include a final shot or scene at the end to create a smooth loop for the video.

''Ex:'' [https://www.instagram.com/p/C4YXRhmywup/ House On The Outlands - Character Poses - The Martins]

''Ex:'' [https://www.instagram.com/p/C7Ml-ZyS6Kj/ Sunny Side Down - Character Poses - Fenga]

===== Compositing Breakdowns =====
For the compositing breakdown videos, we can start with the rough sketch or animatic version, then gradually assemble the different layers and assets to create the final scene or frame, similar to other compositing breakdown videos. Afterward, we can let the scene play out and ensure the video loops smoothly back to the beginning.

Alternatively, we can create compositing breakdown videos without starting with the animatic version. In this approach, we showcase a scene, freeze at a specific frame, and then break down that frame by assembling the various layers, lines, and assets that create the final picture!
