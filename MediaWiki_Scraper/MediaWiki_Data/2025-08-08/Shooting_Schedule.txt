The live shooting document is stored in google drive in your projects folder

If you need to access the template, it can be found in the templates / brand folder
<blockquote>https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/0AC6jQ8G_PA24Uk9PVA</blockquote>

Or in the MAKE Google drive you can navigate to the template file here:
 <nowiki>
Templates / Brand > [ project template ] > Template_Production related </nowiki>

After a shoot has finished and footage sorting/weeding begins, the VFX supervisor or Producer should save this doc as a PDF in the Spot's Project Folder:
