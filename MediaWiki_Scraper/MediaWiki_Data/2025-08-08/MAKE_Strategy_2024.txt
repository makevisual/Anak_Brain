=MAKE Strategy 2024=

==Summary of the organization.==
MAKE is '''a multi-dimensional creation studio that specializes in the art of storytelling through Animation, Visual Effects and Motion Graphics'''. It is a growing company that provides creative, production, and post-production services, as well as creates original short films for a alternative media market.

With nearly '''20 years''' of commercial experience, MAKE has gained a reputation as a trusted creative partner among national and international companies (Mattel, Spinmaster, Hasbro, Callaway, Arctic Cat), brands, and agencies (BBDO, DAVID, Publicis, DentsuMB, Saatchi, Media Monks). At MAKE we take care of a '''full creative process from start to finish'''. 


This implies initial creative ideation, script writing, story development, style definition, shooting, modeling, animation, post-production services, and sound mixing.


Our talented diverse team consists of motion designers, 2D, and 3D animators, VFX artists, illustrators, writers, modelers, and producers. But all of us get to wear different hats based on the project and expertise.


'''What is unique about MAKE:'''

1. Our approach with clients always revolves around understanding the final target audience and the client’s needs.

2. We specialize in high-end complex productions with a high level of intensity and scale of deliverables.

3. No matter the size and complexity of the project we always deliver above expectations.

4. We deliver high-impact videos that captivate audiences, blending cutting-edge animation techniques with compelling storytelling to leave a lasting impression.

== Vision and Mission statements.==
A vision statement details where the organization aspires to be. A mission statement defines the organization's business, its objectives, and how it will reach these objectives. 

Our vision is to '''be a great creative studio'''.

Our mission is to '''create outstanding quality story-based animation and VFX commercial, and original stories while fostering long term client partnerships and communities'''.

== MAKE’s value proposition.==

A value proposition is a statement that summarizes why a client or customer would choose your product or service. Here it is important to differentiate between MAKE Commercial and MAKE Originals brunches as they address different nature of the business as well as the different client/audience.


'''MAKE'''
MAKE helps brands and companies to bring stories and campaigns (through VFX, animation, and motion graphics) to life by providing high-end complex productions and full in-house reliable creative services.

* Experience and proven success with a high level of intensity, scale, and complexity of projects.

* Substituting agencies in the understanding of clients' needs and creative direction.

* MAKE’s productions provide creative freedom and agility to commercial clients.

* MAKE guarantees reliability for quality delivery in a timely manner.

* Speed of execution of commercial projects.


'''MAKE ORIGINALS''' provides easy-to-watch animated micro-short and short films and series for alternative media consumers and sci-fi and fantasy genre lovers.
In addition, MAKE ORIGINALS helps production and distribution companies to create appealing sci-fi and fantasy films by licensing and selling unique creative ideas and stories (IP).

==Strategies.==
Strategies refers to the long-term roadmap for an organization, in other words instructions on how we will achieve out Vision.


The overarching company strategy aims to '''cultivate synergy between MAKE Commercials and Originals, enhancing the studio’s visibility and elevating the quality and complexity of our projects'''.


To do that we will implement the following cyclic (or looping) strategy:

'''Create Original content in order to get bigger and better commercial projects and brand films inline with our goals.'''

and

'''Create bigger and better commercial projects and brand films to get more funds to increase MAKE Original production and develop the studios voice.'''


Additionally, this synergy between MAKE Originals and Commercial branches will result in:

* Enhancing creative thinking abilities.

* Elevating screenwriting skills.

* Refining character development processes.

* Optimizing R&D procedures.

* Establishing credibility in design and ideation phases.

* Cultivating an internal team of directors and lead artists.

* Experimenting with artistic direction.

* Building a brand.

* Building relationships with outside creatives.

* Attracting outstanding talent.

* Nurturing a sense of happiness and pride in one's work.

* And More…

==Industry Insights.==

In today's dynamic landscape of content consumption, the entertainment industry has witnessed a paradigm shift marked by an increase in personalized content, polarization, and the emergence of information bubbles. Consumers now crave not only high-quality animated content but also content that caters to their individual preferences and interests.


The rise of streaming services has transformed how audiences engage with animation, VFX, and games content. However, these platforms find themselves introducing an increasing number of advertisements to their content to compensate the decreasing number of premium subscribers, navigating the delicate balance between user experience and revenue generation. Simultaneously, these streaming giants face stiff competition from alternative and free platforms such as IGTV and TikTok, creating a dynamic landscape where content distribution strategies are continually evolving. 


Content creators and providers must adapt to the evolving preferences of consumers while navigating the challenges posed by volatile economic landscape that impacts consumer expectations and behavior. 

==Key Trends (2023-2024).==

'''Major platforms like Instagram and YouTube feel like a wasteland.'''

In non-financial terms, major platforms often feel like a wasteland — and increasingly, their users are just repackaging content from TikTok or X. Big platforms are losing the cultural game, even if they don’t have the metrics to measure it.


'''Further decrease in spending on original content.'''

Streaming services are strategically shifting their approach, opting to reduce investments in original productions. Instead, they are redirecting their focus towards third-party content and licensing deals, particularly targeting content with a robust existing fan base. Although total content spending for subscription streaming services is anticipated to maintain an upward trajectory, the pace of increase is expected to slow down.


'''The top challenge for the media industry in a slowing economy is revenue.'''

Media companies’ top-line revenue is decreasing as consumer habits shift, while bottom-line costs are increasing based on content costs. Saturation in the media industry is making it harder for companies to grow user counts, especially in the streaming sector which is also being affected by inflation. As consumer spending gets squeezed by inflation, streaming subscribers are becoming more and more fickle. Consumers are faced with more platform choices than ever and are making more deliberate decisions to cancel existing subscriptions when they start new ones. 


'''Brand persona is crucial.'''

Recent research from IAB shows that 39% of consumers are watching more creator content than they were just a year ago. The data also shows that they’re more likely to seek information on a topic or engage with it further after watching creator content compared with studio content. In addition, with the rise of AI, creating truly original content is critical for success. 


'''Virtual Production.'''

Virtual production allows real-world and digital platforms to converge using a game engine to create virtual sets and environments during filmmaking. It allows for more flexibility and creativity in filmmaking, as it eliminates the need for physically building every set or flying crew to physical locations. It also allows for some of the filmmaking processes to take place virtually, allowing people to work remotely.


'''AI and creativity.'''

Machine learning and AI technologies are being used in a variety of ways, from automating tasks to creating more realistic simulations. Democratization of these technologies are likely to continue to advance and become more integrated into the animation and creation pipelines in the coming years.


'''Rise of Multi-Platform Consumption.'''

Consumers increasingly access content across multiple devices and platforms, leading to a more integrated media experience. This trend reshapes how we distribute and consume content, focusing on seamless, cross-platform accessibility.


'''Production company consolidation under larger holding companies.'''

Continuing trend of production companies’s consolidation under bigger companies. (Ex.: Technicolor - The Mill, residence - Buck).
