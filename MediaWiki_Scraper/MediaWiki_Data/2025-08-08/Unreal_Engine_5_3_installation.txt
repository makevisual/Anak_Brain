[[category:IT]]

* Download Epic Games Launcher: https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/download
* Navigate to the 'Library' tab in un Unreal Engine section.

[[File:Image.png|none|thumb|581x581px]]

* Click the '+' Next to engine versions and select the appropriate Engine Version, currently 5.3.0
* [[File:Engines.png|frameless]]
*

* Click the 'Install' button on your new engine version, and wait until setup is complete. Around 30 minutes.
