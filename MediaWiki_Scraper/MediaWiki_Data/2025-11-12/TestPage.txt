== Header1 ==
Each time you add an additional indent, the style changes slightly.
=== Header2 ===
you can keep doing this for quite a while.
==== Header3 ====
Each header has its own link in the navigation panel at the top of the article.
===== Header4 =====
Eventually the headers become difficult to tell apart, and you may be better off just using bullet points, or sub-items

=Sub-Items=
Sub-Items work the same way as Headers, however their formatting stays the same.
*You can keep adding sub-items just as you would with headers.
**But unlike with headers, each item should be the content.
***So that the formatting looks clean, you would want to follow this structure.
****And you can go for quite a while.

=Media=

What good is a media wiki without the media? It is pretty simple to imbed pictures and video into your page.

First you will need to upload some media. You can do so by finding the "upload file" link under the "tools" headder on the left hand side toolbar of most pages on this mediawiki.

Below you will see some examples of inline content, and the same content displayed as a thumbnail.

==Inline Media==

This is inline media, and can have its width set by the number after the pipe character in the link. Look at the source code to see how to add an image.

[[File:HankHillHotdog.jpg|250px]]

And here is how to add a video.

[[File:Hotdogvideo.mp4|250px]]

==Thumbnail Content==

[[File:Hotdogvideo.mp4|thumb]]

Additionally content can be shown as a thumbnail, that is off to the right of the page, staying out of way of the main content you wish to provide. Here you can see the text 'thumb' is used after the pipe, to define the media as a thumbnail. Both of these instances refer to the same file, and if replaced, or updated, would update all instances across all pages. This can be done for images and video.

You can control the media much much more, with captions, borders, and other placement and size controls. If you look at this link it will show you [https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Help:Images#Syntax|all available syntax.]
