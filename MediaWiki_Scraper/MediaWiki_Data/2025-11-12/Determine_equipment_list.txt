

'''<u><big>General Information</big></u>''' 

Every shoot has different equipment needs that are based on where a shoot takes place, the subject and the desired look. 

The equipment list for a shoot is finalized during the Crew Pre Production meeting that takes place before Prelight. 

This meeting always includes the Director, Producer(s), VFX, DP, Key Gaffer and Key Grip. 

Depending on the specifics of the shoot this meeting could also include: 1st AC, Toy Tech, Art Department, Production Coordinator etc.

'''<big><u>General Equipment List Steps for Three Types of Shoots</u></big>''' 

# <big>'''On-Site Shoot'''</big> (@ MAKE)
#* [[2024 MAKE Stage Inventory]] and Project Boards/Animatics are shared with Crew before Crew Pre Production Meeting. 
#* During Crew Pre Production meeting director will go through all shots to discuss how the desired effect will be accomplished, using what specific Camera, G&E, etc. equipment.  
#* Item(s) determined necessary that are not on the [[2024 MAKE Stage Inventory]] are added to list of items for rental. This rental list is contributed to and compiled in an email between Producer, DP, Gaffer, Grip. 
#* Final equipment rental list is then sent to rental vendor and a price quote is requested.
#* Price quote is shared with Creative Director prior to prelight for approval. 
# <big>'''Off-Site Studio Shoot'''</big>
#* The same steps are followed as for an On-Site Shoot except: 
#** During the Crew Pre Production Meeting it is decided if and what <u>MAKE owned Camera, Camera equipment, Lenses and/or Lights</u> will be brought to the Off-Site Studio.   '''''NOTE'''''  *** No MAKE owned G&E equipment is brought to Off-Site Studios. All G&E equipment is rented. ***
#** [[MAKE Gear Package]] list is used to Check equipment OUT before it is taken to the Off-Site Studio and checked back IN when it returns to MAKE after the shoot.
# '''<big>Location Shoot</big>'''
#* The same steps are followed as for an Off-Site Studio Shoot except: 
#** Equipment Rental discussion and rental list creation will largely take place during the Location Tech Scout.
#** Additional production equipment and rentals including transportation, tables, chairs, etc. will be needed. Line Producer/Production Coordinatior is in charge of these items.
