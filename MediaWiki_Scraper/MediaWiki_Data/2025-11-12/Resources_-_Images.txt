=How to use=
Browse the following locations for desired images, and then copy them into your working project, so all the assets are within the directory.


=Where to find images=
You can find many images in U drive to use for textures 
 <nowiki>
U:\resources\__Textures</nowiki>

and HDRI images can be found in here
 <nowiki>
U:\resources\__HDRI</nowiki>


Images are not very organized in U drive so it could be easier to download new textures/hdri from sites like

https://polyhaven.com/


or grab textures from already made models in
 <nowiki>
U:\resources\Models</nowiki>


We also have account in Shutterstock so if you need images from the site, ask producer, director of the spot

https://www.shutterstock.com/

=Where to save images=

Always copy over your textures to project you are working on. Do not link to files in U drive


They can either go into project's images folder (ex/ W:\Projects\BraveHurts\Assets\Images) 


or "Surfaces" folder of Specific asset you are working on. (ex/ W:\Projects\BraveHurts\Assets\Characters\DAM_Damsel\Surfaces)


When referencing information from this file or document, link back to this URL: https://makevisual.com/brain/mediawiki/index.php?title=Resources_-_Images
