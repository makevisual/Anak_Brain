=What to expect=
We have several collections of stock footage elements that can include effects, light leaks, lens flares, dust, fog, and various items that can be integrated into a shot or edit.

=Where to find stock footage=
You can find many stock footages in

 <nowiki>

 <nowiki>

 <nowiki>

https://www.shutterstock.com

=Where to save stock footage=

'''Always''' copy over your footage to project you are working on. Do not link to files in U drive. This is because a project will be archived, and all the assets that are a part of it should go along with it.

They can either go into project's footage folder (ex/ W:\Projects\BraveHurts\Assets\Footage)

or "footage" folder of Specific shot you are working on. (ex/ W:\Projects\BraveHurts\SPOT__Catapult_20s\S_BH-CAT_010\Footage)
