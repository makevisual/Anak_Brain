=Process=
'''Step By Step'''

On the left side of Wrike, find the three dots that say '''"More"''' and click '''"Calendars."'''

[[File:How to make a schedule for Roto vendor 1.mp4|frameless]]

In the calendar page click on '''"+ New Calendar."''' Name the calendar Sancio followed by whatever the current season we working for and year.

[[File:How to make a schedule for Roto vendor 2.mp4|frameless]]

After the calendar is created click the three dots and select '''"Create Layer"''' and name the layer '''"Sending/Receiving."'''

[[File:How to make a schedule for Roto vendor 3.mp4|frameless]] 

Click '''"+Select"''' and Type '''"Schedule -"''' and search the name of the project(s). '''*Typing "Schedule -" will help you find your proj folder easier*'''

[[File:How to make a schedule for Roto vendor 4.mp4|frameless]]

Click on the '''"Status"''' drop arrow and select '''"Workflow"''' then '''"Scheduling."''' Scroll up and select '''"Send Vendor"''' and '''"Receive Vendor."'''

[[File:How to make a schedule for Roto vendor 5.mp4|frameless]]

After, click on the three dots for '''"Sending/Receiving"''' hit share and type '''"MAKE."'''
