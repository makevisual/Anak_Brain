1) Have your Photoshop project open.

2) Select '''File''' from the upper left corner of the window.

3) Select '''Export.'''

4) Choose '''Render Video.'''

5) Make sure in the dropdown panel, '''Photoshop Image Sequence''' is selected and not Adobe Media Encoder.

6) Set '''Format to PNG'''.

7) '''Name and save your file''' to its appropriate location.

8) Hit '''Render.'''
