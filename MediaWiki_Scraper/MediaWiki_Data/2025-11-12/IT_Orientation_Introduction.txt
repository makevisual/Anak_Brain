===Where to save files===
All of your working files should be stored on the network, there is a location for anything that you should need. Saving files onto your local computer is dangerous because computers may get swapped, interchanged, have hardware failures, or require maintenance at any time. Being able to switch to a different workstation and continue work is only possible if everything was saved safely on the appropriate network location. The network is redundant, and backed up frequently, so there is a significantly lower chance of data loss.

====Basic Troubleshooting====

If you are having an issue connecting to the internet, the network or obtaining a software license, you should always follow these steps, before asking someone for help.

 <nowiki>
restart software, sign out and back in, restart computer</nowiki>

If something in your DCC (Digital content creation) software is not working not working, you should always follow these steps, before asking someone for help.

 <nowiki>
restart software, sign out and back in, restart computer</nowiki>

If you are having issues with audio or video for Google Chat or zoom calls, , you should always follow these steps, before asking someone for help.

 <nowiki>
restart software, sign out and back in, restart computer</nowiki>

If you computers local hard drive is filling up, please let someone know if the problem persists after following these steps.

 <nowiki>
restart computer</nowiki>

If you have a problem, don't wait until the last min before something is due to request a fix, or help. If you have been having issues with your microphone or speakers, attempt to fix, or request help immediately after you experience the problem, do not wait until right before the next meeting or call you have. If you computers local hard drive is filling up, please let someone know and they will be able to help you at their next availability.

If there is an emergency and you need to work and are unable, most of the computers here are roughly equivalent, and if your project is properly stored on the network, you can switch to another workstation to continue work until a solution is available.
