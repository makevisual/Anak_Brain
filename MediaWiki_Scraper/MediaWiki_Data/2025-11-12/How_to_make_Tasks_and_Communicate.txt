This is an article about to make a task, update a task, and communicate within Wrike.

===  1. Click the + button and name the task you're making. ===
[[File:WrikeTask101 Part1.mov|none|thumb|1920x1920px|1. Click the + button and name the task you're making.]]

=== 2. Write the description for the task. ===
[[File:WrikeTask101 Part2.mov|none|thumb|1920x1920px|2. Write the description for the task.]]

=== 3. Assign the task to a person. ===
[[File:WrikeTask101 Part3.mov|none|thumb|1920x1920px|3. Assign the task to a person.]]

=== 4. Fill in the date for the task. ===
[[File:WrikeTask101 Part4.mov|none|thumb|1920x1920px|4. Fill in the date for the task.]]

=== 5. Communicating on Wrike is similar to communicating via social media. Type @ followed by a name. You can also use @assignees to communicate with everyone added to the task. You can reply to comments, add photos and video, and reply with emojis. ===
[[File:WrikeTask101 Part5.mov|none|thumb|1920x1920px|5. Communicating on Wrike is similar to communicating via social media. Type @ followed by a name. You can also use @assignees to communicate with everyone added to the task. You can reply to comments, add photos and video, and reply with emojis.]]

=== 6. To update a task you edit the status and change to whatever the progress of the task is. ===
[[File:WrikeTask101 Part7.mov|alt=|none|thumb|1920x1920px|6. To update a task you edit the status and change to whatever the progress of the task is.]]
