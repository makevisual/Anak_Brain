=Overview=

In this article you will learn the following:
* how to find and use your license for adobe creative cloud
* what the different types of licenses are, and what they are used for
* how to reassign and swap licenses as needed, for those with appropriate privilege

==Finding your License==
Each individual that receives an Adobe login credential, will be able to find it in their named user folder in Wrike.
To locate yours, go to your -NAME folder, in the "Shared with me" section of Wrike.
You will find a folder inside, labeled "your name" - IT info

[[File:Adobe_Creative_Cloud_Licensing_01.png|350px]]

In this folder you may see a variety of items, including your credentials, and some information about your workstation or computer setup.

[[File:Adobe_Creative_Cloud_Licensing_02.png|950px]]

Select your Adobe CC License task ( noted by the license status ) and you will see your login information.

==License Usage Message==

Each account can sign in, in two locations simultaneously, before you switch to a new computer, please sign out of adobe creative cloud, to minimize these issues.

If too many computers are signed in simultaneously, you will receive an error, and be requested to sign a computer out. It will look like this:

[[File:Adobe_Creative_Cloud_Licensing_03.png|550px]]

If this is the case, speak with a producer or IT person to be able to tell you which machine to 'boot' out.

This is why it is important for everyone to use their assigned licenses, and to stay consistent with updating Wrike with the status of who is using what.

==Types of licenses==

We have two tiers of creative cloud licenses, which are listed in your license task in Wrike, and they offer different access to applications

*Adobe CC License - 
**This is the full standard suite of Adobe applications ( photoshop, premiere, after effects, etc... )

*Adobe CC License + Substance Applications - 
**This is the full standard suite of Adobe applications ( photoshop, premiere, after effects, etc... ) Plus the addition of the Substance suite of 3D tools. These are fewer in count and should be used by 3D team members.

==Assigning / swapping licenses==

If you find yourself without a license, or needing to swap, please speak to a ''producer'' who will have access to the space on Wrike to be able to find availability, and re-assign licenses to appropriate individuals.

===How to Assign / swap licenses===

If you have access to 'The Team' space in Wrike, you will be able to re-assign licenses.

Go to 'The Team' space in wrike, and select the 'Adobe License Tasks' folder.
From there you will be able to see the list of available licenses, and who has them assigned to them.

[[File:Adobe_Creative_Cloud_Licensing_04.png|750px]]

To assign / re-assign a license, select one that does not have more than 1 person assigned ( two is the maximum ).
Assign the users IT folder as a parent folder in Wrike.
Ensure no task is assigned to more than 2 people at once.

[[File:Adobe_Creative_Cloud_Licensing_05.png|750px]]

It is good practice to message the individuals effected on the task so they are aware of what has changed as well.
