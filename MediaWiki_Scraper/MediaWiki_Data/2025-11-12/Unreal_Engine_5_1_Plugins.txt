== '''Vive Mocap Kit:''' ==
This plugin is used to integrate Vive trackers with [https://store.steampowered.com/app/250820/SteamVR/ Steam VR] and Unreal and use them with IK-Based remapping to do full-body motion capture in real-time. Also exportable as FBX.
 https://www.unrealengine.com/marketplace/en-US/product/vive-mocap-kit

 Project resources and docs: U:\resources\__Domains\Plugins\Vive Mocap Kit

== '''Faceware Live Link:''' ==
Used to interpret [http://support.facewaretech.com/studio-intro Faceware Studio] facial animation/control data and apply it to characters/objects in Unreal.
 http://support.facewaretech.com/live-link-plugin
