We are a multidisciplinary and adaptive workplace where multiple people where multiple hats, some individuals fit in multiple groups or can change roles depending on the project, however here are some broad strokes to help you understand what the roles are, and when it is best to seek out which role, for which issue.

==Directors==
For any given project a director will be attached, they will be able to tell you how to creatively solve the problem and have the picture of the piece in their mind and can give appropriate direction. Directors can answer the "Why" questions for you most easily. Why is this character saying this and how should their performance reflect that.

==Production==
Producers work with the client and our team to plan and execute the project. They most often have the details of what the client wants, formats, due dates, and other specific details. Producers can answer the "What" questions for you most easily. Like what am I making, what is the resolution, what is the duration etc...

==Leads==
Leads artists will also be attached to each project. Leads can answer the "How" for your questions. How can a problem be solved, which shots should I be working on, in which order. They are hands on and are involved in the projects production. 

==Artists==
Artists are involved in the production of shots and content. They will work with their producer, director, and lead to accomplish their shots, assets, or other production related items.

==Mentors==
Each new hire, or intern, will be assigned a Mentor, that is the individual the new hire/intern should approach first when they have a question, and they should be working closely with their Mentor to develop their skills and knowledge, as well as the Mentor taking active interest in their mentee.

==Interns==
Interns are the new jolt of energy and excitement that we have the privilege of hosting, and will typically be working on a Days project, and also working closely with their Mentor in production when able.

==Marketing==
Individuals who present the work created to the world and do their best to make sure it gets seen by as many people as possible, additionally they bring in new clients for us to work with on future projects.

==Misc==
There of course are other roles like IT, Strategy, and Office coordinator, as well as some others that may have been overlooked. These people you will usually be pointed to by your Mentor or Lead as needed for special cases.

==Owner==
Danny is the owner of MAKE, and chances are you know who he is already. If you have been unable to get your issue addressed elsewhere, he should be able to help you out.
